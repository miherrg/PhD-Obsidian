# Linear and Pure Resolutions — References

#commutative-algebra #resolutions #syzygies

---

## Books

- **Bruns, W. & Herzog, J.** — *Cohen-Macaulay Rings*. Cambridge University Press. Standard reference for free resolutions; covers pure resolutions in the Cohen-Macaulay setting.

- **Miller, E. & Sturmfels, B.** — *Combinatorial Commutative Algebra*. Springer. Great for linear resolutions arising from simplicial complexes and squarefree ideals.

- **Peeva, I.** — *Graded Syzygies*. Springer. Modern, comprehensive treatment of free resolutions including linear ones.

---

## Key Papers

- **Eisenbud, D. & Schreyer, F.-O.** (2009) — *The Existence of Pure Free Resolutions*. [arXiv:0709.1529](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0709.1529). Foundational paper constructing pure resolutions of every possible Betti diagram type.

- **Boij, M. & Söderberg, J.** — Conjectural framework (proved by Eisenbud–Schreyer) on the cone of Betti diagrams. Entry point: [Boij–Söderberg Reading List (CMI)](https://www.cmi.ac.in/~syz2012/BoijSoederbergReadingList.pdf).

- **Bruns, W. & Conca, A.** — *Linear Resolutions of Powers and Products*. [PDF](https://www.home.uni-osnabrueck.de/wbruns/brunsw/pdf-article/Singularities+and+Computer+Algebra-3.pdf). Focuses on conditions under which powers of ideals have linear resolutions.

- **Fløystad, G., McCullough, J. & Peeva, I.** — *Three Themes of Syzygies*. [PDF](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~irena/papers/threeRes.pdf). Survey connecting linear resolutions, Castelnuovo–Mumford regularity, and Boij–Söderberg theory.

---

## Classic / Combinatorial Angle

- **Fröberg, R.** (1990) — *On Stanley-Reisner Rings*. Characterizes when edge ideals have linear resolutions via chordal graphs. Classic combinatorial result.
